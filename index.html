01 / WHAT I DO

Designed for people.
Built for complexity.

Thoughtful processes create space for new thinking. I bring structure, creativity, and participation to the questions that matter.

01

Participatory Process Design & Facilitation

+

Helping diverse perspectives become a foundation for collaborative action.

I design and facilitate processes that bring people together to explore complex questions, exchange knowledge, identify shared priorities, and develop solutions.

Services include:

  • Stakeholder engagement and multi-stakeholder dialogue
  • Co-creation workshops and collaborative problem-solving
  • Participatory strategy and vision development
  • Facilitated decision-making and consensus-building
  • Interdisciplinary and cross-sector collaboration
  • Design and moderation of interactive meetings, retreats, and events

Typical outcomes: Shared understanding, clearer priorities, stronger collaboration, and agreed next steps.

02

Experiential Learning, Serious Games & Simulations

+

Making complex systems and challenges tangible through experience.

I develop interactive learning formats that enable participants to experiment with decisions, explore different perspectives, and understand relationships within complex systems.

Services include:

  • Design and facilitation of serious games
  • Role-playing and scenario-based simulations
  • Interactive workshops and learning experiences
  • Customized training and capacity-development programs
  • Experiential learning for interdisciplinary teams
  • Game-based approaches to dialogue and collaborative decision-making

Typical outcomes: Deeper understanding of complex issues, improved dialogue, practical learning, and new perspectives on possible solutions.

03

Research & Methodology Design

+

Turning research questions into meaningful collaborative processes.

I support academic institutions, research consortia, and project teams in developing and applying participatory research methods.

Services include:

  • Participatory and transdisciplinary research design
  • Co-creation methodology development
  • Living Lab and collaborative experimentation formats
  • Stakeholder engagement strategies for research projects
  • Customized methodological toolkits
  • Research workshops and collaborative knowledge integration
  • Evaluation and refinement of participatory methods

Typical outcomes: Well-designed research processes, meaningful stakeholder participation, adaptable methodological tools, and stronger connections between research and practical application.

03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

mm✳
RESEARCH MIND. COLLABORATIVE SPIRIT.

Bridging disciplines.
Connecting people.

I hold a PhD in governance and the socioeconomics of natural resources, alongside advanced academic training in agricultural sciences and resource management.

My professional experience spans international research institutions, interdisciplinary projects, consulting assignments, and educational programs.

Across these roles, I have designed and facilitated participatory processes, developed decision-support frameworks, coordinated collaborative research initiatives, and created experiential learning formats, including serious games and simulations.

My work has involved partnerships with universities, research networks, public institutions, and international organizations.

This combination of scientific training, methodological expertise, and practical project experience allows me to move between research, facilitation, learning, and implementation.
